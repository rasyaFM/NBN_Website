Point-to-point connection

Radio Link

Point-to-point connection refers to a communications connection between two nodes or endpoints. Information sent from one endpoint is intended for the other connected endpoint.

This is contrasted with a point-to-multipoint or broadcast connection, in which many nodes can receive information transmitted by one node.
